Comparison with PR 80

Design choices versus
the existing five-page path.

PR 80 contains the operational overview, watch, fit, next-step and payment-status pages. These new concepts explore how the sales message is presented before choosing a design to integrate.

Page / conceptBest useMain emphasisIntegration status
PR 80 · five pagesOperational funnel with request, suppression, staff approval and verified payment.Detailed scope and carefully separated request, booking and payment states.Existing implementation; launch settings and provider configuration still gated.
01 · The Property FileVisitors who benefit from seeing the package structure.Records, six work areas, DIY versus delegation, repeated fit invitations.Standalone visual and interaction preview.
02 · The Direct ConversationVisitors arriving with context and ready to watch.Video and a short, calm explanation with the offer immediately visible.Standalone visual and interaction preview.
03 · The Records FirstVisitors who need an educational starting point.Overview invitation, optional consent and an ungated route to the video.Signup simulation only; no GetResponse enrollment.
04 · The Guided DecisionVisitors needing more explanation, especially on mobile.Bold video hero, steps, eligibility cards and expanded questions.Standalone visual and interaction preview.
05 · The Guardian of the RecordVisitors considering direct purchase after a full explanation.Archival navy/gold, three record layers, owner-supplied value estimates, proposal-letter bonus and draft testimonial cards.Supplied external checkout/webinar links; no fit-call step or integrated intake.

Recommended starting design: 01. It gives the six work areas room to breathe and makes the choice to delegate clear. Use 02 when traffic already knows the offer; 03 for an overview-first campaign; 04 for a longer mobile explanation. These are design judgments, not conversion test results.
